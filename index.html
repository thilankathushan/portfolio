Sri Lanka Baseball

Official website of Sri Lanka Baseball, the national governing body for the sport. Fans get notices, tournaments, match line scores, team profiles, and a photo gallery. Association staff run everything from a no-code admin panel.

jpura-authentic.web.app
Client · Solo build 2026

J'PURA Authentic

Merchandise store for the University of Sri Jayewardenepura baseball team, selling their limited-edition baseball-style crop top. Customers browse three colors, choose sizes, fill a cart, and place orders that the team tracks and fulfils.

  • Live stock per color and size, decremented in a Firestore transaction so two buyers can't claim the last item
  • Checkout with bank transfer (payment slip upload to private storage) or pay on collection
  • Order export script that turns every order into an Excel sheet with links to its payment slip

Embedded · Team of 3 2026

Smart Blind Stick

An assistive cane that warns visually impaired users about hazards through vibration, helps them find a misplaced stick, and sends their location to a caretaker in an emergency. Everything runs on a single Arduino Nano.

ObstacleUltrasonic, ≤ 50 cm WaterMoisture at the tip DarknessAmbient light sensor HapticsPattern per hazard Find-My-Stick433 MHz RF remote GPS SOSMaps link via SMS
  • Firmware prioritises alerts (obstacle › water › light) so overlapping hazards never blur together
  • Fixed SIM800L current spikes resetting the board with a dedicated supply path, and fit the firmware in 2 KB SRAM
